# Logo

The Huddle mark: an amber-soft rounded square holding a large amber disc and three small dots. It reads as a chat (a speaker and the "…" of a message on the way) and as a friendly robot face, which is the product in one shape: people and an AI in the same conversation.

- `variant="lockup"` (default): the mark plus "huddle" in Inter semibold, lowercase, tracked -0.02em, in `ink`. The gap is 0.3× the mark size and the word is 0.72× the mark size.
- `variant="mark"`: the mark alone, for avatars, favicons and tight spaces.
- `tone="soft"` (default): `amber` shapes on `amber-soft`, for use inside the product.
- `tone="solid"`: the inversion, `amber-soft` shapes on `amber`. This is the app icon, and the version to use at 16–24px, where the soft square fades into light backgrounds.
- Leave clear space of at least a quarter of the mark's size on every side. Don't rotate, outline, add a shadow or recolour it outside the amber tokens.
- The mark is the brand. Inside a chat, the AI is still shown by its `Avatar`, so the logo never stands in for the agent.
- Files for use outside the app are in the Logos asset group.
