# HuddleScreen

The v1 experience, end to end, made only from Huddle components. v1 is one huddle opened from a link, so there's no sidebar, search or invite. Use the State menu at the top to jump to any state in the UI states table, or just use it:

1. Join with a name.
2. Send a message and watch the receipt: clock, ✓, ✓✓, then the amber third tick when the AI has read it. The AI then writes a reply.
3. Open the face pile to pause, resume or rename the AI. Each adds a note to the thread.
4. Switch state to see: loading history, empty huddle, connecting, reconnecting, send failed, AI paused, AI resting (budget) and huddle ended.

The Prototype bar and its Theme menu are for exploring; they aren't part of the product.

Behaviour to notice: when the AI is paused, resting or away, your messages stop at ✓✓ and the third tick doesn't appear until the AI catches up.
